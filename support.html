Is PPC Cards free?
Yes — all six games are free to play with friends or solo. Web access is free for a limited time while we launch, so sign up early. A single optional in-app purchase (Undo unlock) exists in the iOS app.
What is crossplay?
One table, any device. iPhone, Android and PC players join the same matches with the same account.
Where is my account?
If you signed in with Google, your account follows you everywhere - including the Account page on this site. If you played as a guest, your account lives on that device: open the app and link Google in Settings to make it portable.
How do I report a player or a bug?
In-game chat has flag and block built in. For anything else, email us - a human reads every message.
