What is PPC Cards?
Eight multiplayer card games in one app: Pineapple OFC, Classic OFC, Gin Rummy, Rummy 500, Scala 40, Palace, Hearts and Klondike Solitaire. Every game supports live online tables with friends or solo play, all built for crossplay from day one.
Is PPC Cards free?
Yes — all eight games are free to play with friends or solo, and there are no ads. Web access is free for a limited time while we launch, so sign up early. The iOS app includes a single optional in-app purchase (the Undo unlock).
When is the iOS app coming?
The iPhone app is finished and coming to the App Store very soon. It carries the full eight-game suite, uses the same account as the web version, and adds push notifications so you know the moment it’s your turn or a friend invites you to a table.
What is the Undo unlock?
A one-time $1.99 purchase in the iOS app that unlocks move undo in the OFC games — take back card placements before you lock in. Everything else in the app stays free.
How does crossplay work?
One table, any device. iPhone, Android and PC players join the same live matches with the same account. Invite friends from your friends list or share a table code — whoever you’re playing with, on whatever device, you’re in the same game.
Where is my account?
If you signed in with Google, Apple or email, your account follows you everywhere — including the Account page on this site, where you can see your stats and account info. If you played as a guest, your account lives on that device: open the app and link a sign-in method in Settings to make it portable.
How do I report a player or a bug?
In-game chat has flag and block built in. For anything else, email us — a human reads every message, usually the same day.
